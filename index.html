Current recommendation

Start by deciding Sea-to-Sky scenery vs. easy cabin logistics.

  • If the family wants maximum nature payoff inside the drive gate: choose Squamish + Whistler.
  • If the family wants easy waterfront/lake/cabin logistics: choose Hood Canal + Lake Cushman.
  • If the family wants a U.S. mountain-cabin base without border friction: choose Mount Baker / Glacier.
  • If the family wants the most culture and rainy-day flexibility: choose North Vancouver / Vancouver, accepting more city logistics.

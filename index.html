The decision lens

Pick the landscape first: sea cliffs, rainforest, beach, city harbor, or ferry coast.

Then choose among three lodging paths for that destination so one booked-out house does not collapse the whole plan.

01 · Canada · Best overall if border is OK

Squamish + Whistler

Most distinctive nature payoff; more operational friction from border + Hwy 99 traffic.

This is the option that feels least like a local Eastside weekend: fjord views, alpine gondola, Whistler village, and multiple toddler-scaled nature stops.

What changed

Less lodging tunnel vision. More trip clarity.

  • Added two U.S. options so the shortlist is not Canada-only.
  • Each destination now has three 3BR+ lodging paths.
  • Built-in crib is not treated as special because the family can bring a pack-and-play.
  • The visual style is softer, warmer, more editorial, and closer to Farmluxe without copying its branding.

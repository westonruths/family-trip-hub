Map & routes

Three ways to ride from Black Diamond.

The lines are planning overlays, not GPS tracks — cross-check with the Google Maps links below and the official Valley Trail map, and follow posted signs. The Valley Trail is a paved 50+ km shared-use network, so ride conservatively with the trailer.

The pick · south lakes

Village → Rainbow Park → Lakeside or Wayside Park → optional Nita/Alpha Lake extension → back. Parks, bathrooms, food, and obvious turnaround points the whole way.

Longer scenic · north lakes

Lunch & snack stops

Ride an hour, eat, then decide.

From a 10:30 pickup: ride 45–75 minutes, stop for lunch, then extend or turn back. The best lunch stop is whichever one keeps the ride from becoming a forced march.

Aria's trailer check

Green light only if the fit checks pass.

Before leaving the shop

Aria sits upright comfortably, helmet fits snugly, harness holds her securely, trailer attaches cleanly to the adult bike.

Pack in the trailer

Snacks, water/milk, sun hat, sunscreen, light layer, small toy, diaper kit, and wind cover.

On the trail

Keep speed low, stay off singletrack, use bell/voice around walkers, and stop before Aria is over it — every park is a bailout point.

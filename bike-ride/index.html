Trail choice

Ride the Valley Trail, not singletrack.

The official Resort Municipality of Whistler page describes the Valley Trail as a paved shared-use network of more than 50 km connecting Whistler Village with parks and neighborhoods. It has some network gaps and shared-use etiquette, so use the map and ride conservatively with the trailer.

Primary route · Alta / Rainbow / Lakeside

Village → Rainbow Park / Alta Lake → Lakeside or Wayside Park → optional Nita/Alpha Lake extension → return. This is the best family shape because it has parks, lake stops, bathrooms/food nearby, and obvious turnaround points.

Open south-lakes route
Longer scenic route · Lost / Green / Meadow

Village → Lost Lake → Green Lake / Meadow Park → return through the Valley Trail network. Black Diamond lists Green Lake to Meadow Park as a 4-hour Valley Trail ride and says it can be extended with stops.

Open north-lakes route
Do not force · full five-lake circuit

The full lakes loop can become a 25–30+ km day once real routing, park stops, snack breaks, and trailer pace are included. Do this only with an 8-hour rental and a very happy toddler.

Toddler safety call

Family-friendly if the trailer fit checks out at pickup.

Must pass before leaving

Aria can sit upright comfortably, helmet fits snugly, 5-point harness holds her securely, and the trailer attaches cleanly to the adult bike.

Pack for trailer reality

Snacks, water/milk, sun hat, sunscreen, light layer, small toy/book, diaper kit, and a way to shade/cover her if wind picks up.

Ride rules

Keep speed low, avoid singletrack, use bell/voice around pedestrians, stop before Aria is over it, and treat parks/lakes as bailout points.

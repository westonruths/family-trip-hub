Adult comfort check

Keep it leisurely, not performative.

Before leaving the shop

Everyone has a correctly sized bike, helmet, working brakes, and a comfortable saddle height.

Bring

Water, snacks, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a light layer for the breezy/overcast forecast.

On the trail

Stay on the paved Valley Trail, skip singletrack, use bell/voice around walkers, and treat every park as an easy bailout point.
